What we do

Waterproofing services

Every building and leakage problem is different. Explore our services below; the final treatment should be selected after considering the source of water, surface condition, exposure and project requirements.

⌂

Roof & Terrace Waterproofing

Roof and terrace surfaces are exposed to rain, standing water, heat and changing weather. Our approach starts with checking the surface, drainage, cracks, joints and any existing coating. Depending on the condition, the work may include cleaning, repairing weak areas, preparing the surface, treating corners and applying a suitable waterproofing system. Proper preparation and drainage detailing are important parts of a dependable roof treatment.

◈

PU Waterproofing Coating

Polyurethane (PU) coating can create a continuous protective layer on suitable prepared surfaces. The system is selected according to the substrate, exposure, movement and product instructions. Work may include surface cleaning, repairing defects, primer application, detailing around joints and outlets, and applying the coating at the specified coverage and thickness. The final performance depends on correct preparation, curing and maintenance.

↗

PU Injection Grouting

PU injection grouting is used for selected active leakage paths in concrete, cracks and construction joints. After assessing where water is entering, suitable injection points are planned and the material is injected using appropriate equipment. Some PU grouts react with moisture and expand to help seal the water path. The right material and method depend on the crack, water pressure and structural condition.

▦

Bathroom & Toilet Waterproofing

Leakage from bathrooms and toilets may be linked to floor waterproofing, pipe penetrations, wall-floor junctions, tile joints or plumbing defects. The affected area should be assessed before selecting a repair. Depending on the issue, treatment may involve joint sealing, plumbing coordination, removal of damaged finishes or renewing a waterproofing layer before tiles are reinstated. Addressing the source is essential before repairing stains below.

≋

Wall Dampness & Seepage

Damp patches, peeling paint, salt deposits and bubbling plaster can have different causes, including external rain penetration, plumbing leaks, roof junctions or rising moisture. We help assess the visible symptoms and likely water-entry points before discussing a suitable treatment. Depending on the source, work may involve external waterproofing, crack sealing, joint treatment or repair of affected plaster after the moisture source has been addressed.

▧

Brick Bat Coba Waterproofing

Brick Bat Coba is a traditional terrace treatment that uses brick pieces and cement mortar to form a sloped protective layer when suitable for the structure. The work requires attention to the existing slab, levels, rainwater outlets, junctions and curing. A correctly planned slope helps water drain instead of remaining on the roof. The appropriate waterproofing details should be selected according to site conditions and the intended roof finish.

▤

APP Bitumen Membrane

APP modified bitumen membrane systems are used on suitable roofs and other specified surfaces to provide a waterproofing layer. Reliable installation depends on preparing and priming the substrate, setting out the sheets, forming correct overlaps, and detailing outlets, corners, upstands and penetrations. The membrane type and installation method should match the project specification and safety requirements, especially when torch application is used.

▥

Basement & Foundation Waterproofing

Below-ground walls and foundations can be exposed to soil moisture, groundwater and water pressure. A suitable system may use a specified membrane, protective layer and carefully detailed joints, corners and service penetrations. Surface preparation and protection against damage during backfilling are important. The system should be selected for the site conditions and designed drainage requirements; no single membrane is appropriate for every foundation.

▱

Protective & Bituminous Roof Coating

Protective roof coatings can help shield compatible surfaces from weather exposure and moisture. The surface must be sound and clean, with cracks, joints and drainage details treated as required by the selected system. The coating is applied according to the manufacturer’s coverage and curing guidance. Coating selection depends on the roof material, existing layers, sunlight exposure and the type of protection required.

▤

HDPE Membrane Waterproofing

HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene) membrane waterproofing provides a durable barrier against moisture and water ingress when installed on a properly prepared surface. The system requires suitable surface preparation, correct membrane placement, sealed overlaps and carefully detailed corners and pipe penetrations. A protection layer may be needed before backfilling or covering, depending on the project design.

◇

Epoxy Flooring & Floor Coating

Epoxy floor systems can provide a smooth, hard-wearing finish on suitable concrete floors in homes, commercial areas and industrial spaces. Preparation is critical and may include removing weak material, cleaning, grinding and repairing surface defects. The system is selected according to traffic, moisture and intended use, then mixed and applied within its working time. Curing and site access should follow the product instructions.

⌗

Tile Joint Cutting & PU Sealant Filling

Tile joints and movement joints may need careful cleaning and resealing when the existing seal has failed or water is entering through the connection. Depending on the joint, old material may be removed, edges prepared and a compatible PU sealant applied with the correct joint shape and depth. This service is different from replacing a failed waterproofing layer beneath tiles, so the source of leakage should be assessed first.
